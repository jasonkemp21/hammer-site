The mission

Boxing takes discipline

Hammer's Haven exists to save inner-city youth from violence through boxing and mentoring, and to give them the opportunity and the skills to fight their way to a better quality of life.

The goal is for young people to carry the discipline of the sport into everyday life, and to build character while they do it.

“Fighting is violence based on emotions and impulses, while boxing is a sport that teaches discipline and self-control!”Hammer's Haven

Privacy

Privacy policy

What this website collects, why, and who handles it.

What we collect

When you send a message or a request through this site, we receive the details you type: your name, your email address, your phone number if you add it, and your message or request. We use them only to reply to you and to arrange your training.

Where it is kept

Messages and requests are stored in a database run for us by Supabase, so our staff can read and answer them. We keep them only as long as we need them to help you. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Booking and payments

Class booking, accounts and payments are handled by Mindbody, Inc. on its own pages, under Mindbody's privacy policy. This site never asks for or stores card details.

Cookies and storage

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The round timer remembers your settings in your own browser, and nothing about that leaves your device. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your browser's standard request for them.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete anything you have sent us, email info@hammerfitnessandboxing.com or call (312) 573-1950.
